## Scope

The one-page trilingual portfolio (`src/app/[locale]/page.tsx`): /en, /fr, /ar (RTL). Mode: Experience. The work (the route and the products) leads from the first viewport.

## Audience and action

Tech recruiters, engineering managers and freelance clients in Kuwait, the Gulf and Europe, often on a phone. They decide in about five seconds, then email, open LinkedIn or download the CV.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career is a night flight seen from altitude. The globe is the ground the page stands on, not a 3D widget in a box. It refuses the category default of a centred "Hi, I'm" hero with a gradient name and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Night navy ground (#0C1A2C) with a raised surface (#14263D) and chart-white ink (#EEF0EA); reading sections switch to a daylight chart-paper field. Route magenta (#F0609E, #B4246B on paper) is used only for the route and the contact action. Land is drawn as depth-shaded dots, and unreached land is a designed ghost layer. Archivo widened for display, Alexandria for Arabic. Pill controls, square panels, separation by tone steps, no rules or shadows.

STORY: The visitor learns who he is and where he is now (Kuwait City, pulsing), follows the route Tunis → Montréal → France → Kuwait City as the camera flies it and each stop opens its role, sees the live products, then contacts him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name at display size at the start edge, the title on 1–2 balanced lines, tagline, location, primary "View my work" (magenta, magnetic) and "Download CV". The planet rises from the end edge (centred on phone below the copy), its route arcs in magenta, Kuwait City pulsing. Nothing lies over the headline.

FORM: Night flight chart, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list (the globe-as-hero reading), assigned by the roll. seed:e9f9eb3d9b8999fa. Raises kept from declined challengers: designed absence (seven-segment), one accent for the action that matters (creator-hardware bench), the same metadata band on every item (brine calendar), front is summary and opening is detail (window-box shelf), one continuous surface (ebru).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

Scroll-driven camera flight along the route arcs (R3F, Phase 4) with each landing revealing its experience; reduced motion shows a still globe with every arc drawn.
